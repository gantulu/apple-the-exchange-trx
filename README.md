# Apple The Exchange TRX

A mobile-first online store concept for Apple products, designed for customers in Malaysia.

## Overview

**Apple The Exchange TRX** is a responsive e-commerce storefront concept focused on browsing Apple products and shopping with a Malaysia-localized experience. The design direction takes inspiration from the premium retail atmosphere of The Exchange TRX in Kuala Lumpur.

> **Project status:** Initial project documentation. Application features and implementation details may evolve as development progresses.

## Goals

- Deliver a polished, mobile-first shopping experience.
- Showcase Apple product categories in a clear, easy-to-browse catalog.
- Localize the shopping experience for Malaysia, including Malaysian Ringgit (MYR / RM).
- Make product discovery, product details, and cart review straightforward.
- Provide a foundation for checkout and order-management features as the project evolves.

## Product Categories

The catalog may include:

- **iPhone**
- **Mac** — MacBook and other Mac computers
- **iPad**
- **Apple Watch**
- **AirPods**
- **Accessories**

Product names, specifications, availability, and prices should be verified against reliable sources before being presented as official or current.

## Core Shopping Experience

Planned storefront capabilities:

1. **Home** — featured banners, curated collections, and product highlights.
2. **Product catalog** — browse categories and product listings.
3. **Product details** — images, specifications, available options, and pricing.
4. **Search and filtering** — find products by name or category.
5. **Shopping cart** — review selected items and quantities.
6. **Checkout** — a future-ready flow for Malaysian customers.
7. **Responsive mobile UI** — layouts optimized for phone screens.

These are project goals, not a claim that every feature is already implemented.

## Malaysia Localization

- **Primary market:** Malaysia
- **Currency:** Malaysian Ringgit (MYR), displayed as RM
- **Suggested locale:** English (Malaysia), with room for Bahasa Melayu support
- **Regional context:** The Exchange TRX, Kuala Lumpur
- **Shipping and payment:** To be defined according to the actual business and supported providers

## Design Direction

- Premium, minimal, product-first visual hierarchy
